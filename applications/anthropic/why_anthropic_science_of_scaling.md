# Why Anthropic - Science of Scaling Draft

I want to work at Anthropic because the company appears unusually serious about turning compute into reliable intelligence without hiding uncertainty or cutting corners on engineering truth. That combination matters to me more than shipping vague demos or overstated benchmark wins.

My best fit is the `Research Engineer, Science of Scaling` path because the part of ML systems work that I enjoy most is making training and runtime behavior measurable, debuggable, and operationally honest. In MertFormer Titan, the most valuable work was not inventing a giant performance headline. It was building explicit contracts around readiness, fallback behavior, verification, evidence boundaries, and the difference between what the repo can prove now versus what still requires a real trained run.

I am especially motivated by work that sits between systems engineering and research iteration: making experiment loops cleaner, keeping evaluation honest, tightening interfaces around training/evidence flows, and debugging the real bottlenecks that determine whether compute becomes usable signal. That is the style of work I want to do at much higher leverage.

What I would bring is a measured engineering style: I like making existing systems more legible, keeping boundaries explicit, separating proof from presentation, and doing the unglamorous work that lets larger ML systems scale without lying to themselves.
