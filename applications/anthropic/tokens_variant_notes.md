# Tokens Variant Notes

## When To Use This Variant
Use this version only after the primary `Science of Scaling` package is coherent.

## Narrative Shift
- emphasize turning compute into useful training signal
- emphasize token efficiency, experiment discipline, and evidence hygiene
- keep low-bit/runtime work as supporting systems evidence rather than the only story
- treat LiquidRouter and routing ablations as experiment-design evidence, not hype

## Suggested Angle
The strongest bridge into a `Tokens` role is:
- I care about how compute becomes signal
- I build systems that keep training/evidence boundaries explicit
- I think in terms of bottlenecks, manifests, checkpoints, and experiment discipline
- I avoid narrating absent evidence as if it were already measured

## Claim Boundary
Do not narrate token-efficiency superiority until checkpoint-bound evidence exists.
