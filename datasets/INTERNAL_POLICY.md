# Internal Dataset Policy (Stage Sets, Golden Samples)

This document defines the policy for **internal** datasets shipped or referenced by this repository:
- **Stage sets**: locally produced curriculum snapshots (e.g., `datasets/stage*/stage*_data.jsonl`) generated by `scripts/data_pipeline.py`.
- **Golden samples**: small regression prompts (e.g., `datasets/golden_samples.jsonl`) used for sanity checks.

## Classification
- **License/ownership**: Internal (proprietary).
- **Distribution**: Do not publish or share outside the organization unless explicitly approved.

## Content Rules (Must Hold)
- No secrets, tokens, credentials, private keys, or internal URLs.
- No personal data (PII) or sensitive data (health, biometric, etc.).
- No copyrighted or restricted third-party text unless:
  - the source license explicitly allows training/redistribution, and
  - the dataset is documented in `datasets/SOURCES*.md` + `datasets/LICENSES*.md`.

## Provenance & Hashing
- Every training run must pin the **exact** data snapshot:
  - record snapshot metadata + SHA256 in `datasets/hashes.json`
  - keep raw snapshots outside git (`datasets/` is gitignored by default)

## Review Gate
Before any production training run:
- `datasets/LICENSES*.md` must contain **no** `TBD` for training datasets.
- `datasets/hashes.json` must be filled for the exact snapshot used.
