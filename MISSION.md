# Mission

MertFormer Titan exists to deliver an offline-first, edge-native, locally controllable intelligence infrastructure that is useful to good-faith operators under real-world constraints.

## Official Product Sentence
Turkey-serving, offline-first, edge-native, locally integrable intelligence infrastructure.

## Operating Principles
- Public-good and human-benefiting deployment come first.
- Local control, auditability, and reproducibility are mandatory.
- No claim without evidence.
- High-risk decisions require human approval.
- Harmful autonomy, covert surveillance framing, and unsupported capability inflation are out of scope.

## 45K Positioning
The 45K run is the first serious architecture validation run.
It is not the final capability ceiling and it is not a blanket production claim.
