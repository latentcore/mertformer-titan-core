# Sectoral IP Split Framework (Defense vs Civil)

## Purpose
This document defines a negotiation-ready framework to separate rights between:

- Defense/Security-domain usage rights (institution-exclusive)
- Civil/commercial usage rights (retained by the developer/company)

It is designed for technical transfer discussions and must be validated by legal counsel before signature.

## Scope
Applicable to MertFormer Titan architecture, related code, and derivative deployments.

Out of scope:

- Classified data handling procedures
- Country-specific procurement law details
- Final legal language for binding contracts

## Recommended Rights Split

### 1. Defense Domain (Institution-Exclusive License or Assignment)

- Grant institution-exclusive rights for defense/security use cases.
- Include source access, deployment, adaptation, and internal derivative rights for defense context.
- Restrict re-sale/transfer outside agreed defense perimeter unless separately approved.

### 2. Civil Domain (Rights Retained by Developer)

- Developer retains rights for civilian products:
  - Consumer apps
  - Education
  - Commercial non-defense software
- Civil distribution and productization remain under developer control.

### 3. Transition and Responsibility Transfer

- Define a fixed transition support window (e.g., 3 months).
- After transition acceptance, operational ownership transfers to institution.
- Developer support beyond transition is subject to separate SLA/contract.

## Commercial Structure (Template)

- Technology transfer fee (one-time)
- Optional maintenance package (time-bound)
- Optional integration package (milestone-based)

## Minimum Contract Clauses Checklist

- Domain definitions (defense vs civil)
- Exclusivity boundaries
- Derivative works ownership boundaries
- Confidentiality and data handling
- Warranty/liability limits
- Transition acceptance criteria
- Post-transition support terms
- Termination and dispute resolution
